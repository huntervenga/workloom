# Time off and work capacity

**Feature contract and implementation in progress.** This document describes the new target and the pure calculation boundary. It is not a release certificate or evidence of an activated main runtime. The previous accepted local product remains [90](90-inline-editing-compact-pane-and-undo.md) until a separate frozen candidate, database, browser and activation gate is recorded.

## User model

Time off belongs to a workspace and its people, independently of projects, task estimates and mandatory planning weeks. A person chooses an explicit absence type and inclusive start/end dates. The first release supports whole-day requests; accrued balances may contain fractions, but half-day requests are not silently introduced.

The owner can apply an editable starter template: 21 vacation days/year with unlimited carryover, 10 flexible days/year without carryover, an unpaid absence type, and optional annual December 31 / January 1 company holidays. Those names, counts, dates and behaviors are settings. The calculation engine contains none of these employer-specific defaults. Removing company holidays makes those dates ordinary scheduled days; company holidays do not debit an absence pool. Flexible days are never silently substituted for vacation: the request records its chosen stable type ID.

Each type has a display name/color, annual entitlement, daily or annual-upfront accrual, carryover rule, optional carry expiry, optional negative-balance limit, approval requirement, and independent `consumesBalance`, `pausesAccrual` and `capacityUnavailable` flags. An unpaid type normally consumes no paid balance, pauses accrual of every balance-accruing type, and removes work capacity. A balance-free paid company holiday is a calendar setting rather than a debit against vacation.

## Effective policies and employment

Workspace settings keep year-effective policy versions. The latest version whose `effectiveYear` is no later than the accounting year applies. A new annual rate does not silently rewrite previous years. A closing year's carryover rule determines what that year's remaining balance carries into the next year. Administrative edits of a current or previous policy year need a reason, an explicit recalculation preview and append-only history at the service boundary. Editing a type must preserve its stable identity; historic types remain present as archived definitions in later policies; removing them would silently strand a carried balance and is rejected.

The employee profile records employment start/end, accounting start, opening balances by type, timezone and effective-dated weekly schedules. The default schedule is configurable in settings; the starter is Monday–Friday, 480 minutes/day. Every scheduled day consumes one full leave day regardless of whether that day's work schedule is 4h or 8h; capacity uses the actual scheduled minutes. Employment end is inclusive. Accounting start cannot precede employment or a known policy year. Opening balances mean the balance immediately before accrual on accounting start; earlier employment is not accrued again. The profile editor accepts nonnegative opening buffers. Recording an initial debt uses a separate explicit employer adjustment with a reason, negative approval and an enabled policy/limit, rather than bypassing approval through a negative opening buffer.

The service obtains the current instant from PostgreSQL and derives each person's local date in their recorded timezone (workspace fallback is resolved when creating a profile). Pure functions receive explicit trusted `asOf` dates; browser clocks are not authoritative. Calendar dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`, are inclusive, and never shift through timestamp conversion. Annual February 29 holidays apply only in leap years; impossible month/day combinations are rejected.

## Exact balance calculation

Balances use integer microdays: 1 day = 1,000,000 microdays. Annual entitlement and adjustments are safe bounded integers. Daily accrual uses integer arithmetic:

`floor(annual entitlement × eligible service calendar days / actual days in the year)`.

The engine calculates differences of these cumulative amounts, so daily rounding never compounds and a complete eligible year earns exactly its configured entitlement. Employment, accounting cutover and approved accrual-pausing absences clip eligibility. Ordinary weekends, paid vacation and company holidays remain service days for accrual. An approved unpaid interval pauses every calendar service day in its range, including weekends; overlapping pause ranges are unioned before counting.

Annual-upfront is a separate configurable method. On the first eligible service day of the accounting year, it credits the full year's eligible share, clipped to employment/accounting dates and all currently approved pause ranges in that year. It credits once, not once per calculation date. Entering or cancelling an approved unpaid interval recalculates this amount as well as daily accrual. A retrospective correction can therefore expose a historic shortage; it must not silently delete a confirmed booking or falsify an old report snapshot.

Opening balances, earned accrual, explicit adjustments, taken days, expired days, available balance and future reservations stay separate. Confirmed request allocations store work date, microdays and scheduled minutes at approval. Later holiday or schedule changes do not silently reprice those allocations. When an approved future work date arrives, the same allocation moves from reserved to taken, rather than being debited a second time.

Within the selected pool, consumption uses the earliest expiring carried balance first. No-carry rules expire unused positive balances at the year boundary. Capped carry limits the retained positive amount and preserves the longest-lived remaining balance; subsequent consumption still spends earliest expiry first. Optional carry expiry is exclusive on the first day after the configured number of months from January 1: three months expires April 1. Existing earlier expiries are not extended by another rollover. Expired lots are removed before applying a new carry cap; negative debt is not forgiven by an expiring positive entitlement.

`projectedAvailableMicrodays` is the simulated balance on the last approved future charge date (or `asOf` if there are none), including accrual through that date. The response includes `projectedThroughDate` and the UI must display that horizon. It is not today's earned balance minus reservations. The UI must distinguish these concepts and must not label valid future reservations as current debt.

## Approval, corrections and audit

The charge preview lists scheduled weekdays excluding configured company holidays and exposes intersecting pending/approved requests. New overlapping requests are rejected by the service; calculation of availability defensively unions existing overlaps. Full-day requests are explicit even when their interval contains weekends or holidays.

Approval checks the candidate alongside every existing approved future booking at each charged work date. A positive total at year end is not sufficient if an earlier booking would be uncovered. Negative usage requires both the applicable type policy (including its configured limit) and an explicit authorized negative approval. The default template forbids it. Approval of an accrual-pausing absence validates all affected paid pools, rather than only the balance-free unpaid type. Annual-upfront recalculation checks from the affected January 1 and current debt as well as future charge dates, because a later unpaid interval can reduce the year's upfront credit.

Employees can create and change future days only. Today's/past allocations are locked. For an approved type that requires approval, an employee can only shorten the existing interval; moving it outside its approved dates or adding days requires a new request and employer approval. For an ongoing absence, changing the future tail preserves already confirmed historical day allocations; an administrator may correct history with a reason and a new immutable revision. Past unpaid intervals added by an administrator recalculate earned accrual across all affected pools. No request history, ledger event or prior report snapshot is silently overwritten. Changing settings, employment profiles or schedules also requires a source-fresh preview and revalidation where it affects confirmed bookings. Authorization, transaction barriers, idempotency and audit are service responsibilities, outside the pure engine. Request/bulk previews return an opaque source version; optional write preconditions bind the shown preview after cached-replay/current-target authorization, so an exact retry retains its original body and key. Balance adjustments return their committed DTO and then refresh the overview, keeping response parsing failures distinct from a failed write.

## Availability and reports

For every person/date the engine exposes scheduled minutes, company-holiday minutes, absence minutes and available minutes. Company holidays and approved capacity-removing absences form a union: a holiday during an absence never removes the same capacity twice. Capacity is clipped to employment, independent of task dates, task completion and whether that person has any tasks in a report.

A normal 8h Monday–Friday week has 2,400 available minutes. One full workday off leaves 1,920 minutes. A long unpaid interval removes work capacity and pauses accrual; an ordinary vacation removes capacity without automatically pausing accrual. Gantt and team calendars show neutral unavailable ranges; private request notes and absence reasons do not belong in general project availability responses.

Generic reports retain their existing ordinary custom-field configuration from [86](86-generic-fields-and-reports.md). Plan/fact duration fields remain user-selected fields; capacity is an optional, explicit date-range source. No mandatory week or special Hours field returns. Task-level actual hours without work dates cannot be invented as daily work logs. Capacity source revisions must be included in freshness hashes and stored report snapshots; public capacity replies expose an opaque digest and neutral daily inputs, without raw private request/profile revision lists. Direct queries for former members are manager-only; a saved report's explicitly selected former members are represented neutrally and consistently for its authorized workspace readers. Previously saved snapshots retain their original result after a later unpaid correction.

## Pure interfaces and verification boundary

Contracts: `packages/contracts/src/leave.ts`. Pure modules: `packages/domain/src/leave/calendar.ts`, `calculation.ts`, `accrual.ts`.

- `assertLeaveSource` validates shape, IDs/references, policy-year uniqueness, holiday validity, profile dates, schedules and Temporal timezones.
- `policyVersionForYear` resolves inherited annual rules.
- `previewLeaveCharge` calculates full-day allocations and overlap IDs; it never silently chooses another pool.
- `calculateAvailability` returns neutral per-person/day capacity.
- `calculateLeaveBalance` replays opening balances, accrual, adjustments, carry/expiry and immutable confirmed charges.
- `validateProjectedBalance` checks every future charge date in the supplied approved source; the caller inserts its candidate before calling it and enforces negative-approval results. `validateProjectedChange` compares before/after sources across all paid pools and reports new, deeper or newly forbidden deficits, attributed to explicit approval of the causative write without rewriting old bookings. Each deficit includes its owning request ID.
- `assertConsistentLeaveType` requires a request to be split at a year boundary if its approval snapshot flags change.

Dates before the first known policy are explicitly unsupported for historical capacity calculations: the engine does not invent holiday-free history. Dates outside employment have zero scheduled capacity. Pure request preview and calculation ranges are bounded to 3,660 inclusive calendar days. The HTTP capacity/report/Gantt availability interface is separately bounded to 366 inclusive days and 100 members, so the pure limit does not promise ten-year API reports. Dates are bounded to 1900–2200; service source/profile/request limits also bound calculation work. The engine merges pause intervals, caches annual type rules and coalesces balance lots; it does not allocate one balance lot for every daily accrual.
